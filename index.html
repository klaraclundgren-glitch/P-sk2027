Getting there

Flights to Geneva

Geneva is about 1 h 15 from Chamonix. Both cities have direct flights, but Easter is peak season, and your dates match Norway's påskeferie exactly.

Out Sat 20 MarchThe first day of Norway's Easter week, so the busiest day to leave Oslo. A Friday evening flight is often cheaper and gives you a full ski day on the 20th.
Home Mon 29 MarchEaster Monday is a holiday in Norway, Sweden and France, so return fares peak. Tuesday 30 March is usually cheaper and gives a spare weather day.

Availability

Booking and what's open

Easter is the busiest weekend of the spring touring season, and the Haute Route huts fill first. Hut nights from Thursday 25 to Sunday 28 March are the hardest to get.

Grands MontetsNew Argentière–Lognan gondola due for Christmas 2026; Plan Joran is the backup. Area open daily 5 Dec 2026 – 2 May 2027. Summit lift back in Dec 2027.
Chamonix liftsSki season 19 Dec 2026 – 2 May 2027. The Aiguille du Midi and the Skyway run in winter but stop in strong wind.
Avalanche bulletinsMétéo-France BERA for the Mont-Blanc massif, SLF for Valais and the Aosta Valley bulletin for Courmayeur. The Office de Haute Montagne in Chamonix has daily conditions.
